# The screensaver spells this out one line at a time, about every ten seconds,
# and carries on from where it stopped. This is original text in the manner of
# the writing on the tower wall; it is not quoted from the book.
#
# To use the real passage (or anything else), put it in
#   ~/.config/omarchy/annihilation-passage.txt
# one part per line. That file replaces this one, and lines too long for the
# screen are broken at word boundaries.
Where lies the strangling fruit, there the light remembers its roots.
What was buried has learned the patience of water.
The seeds of the sleeping are shared among the patient dark.
Each hollow fills with a slow green fire.
It speaks in the language of moss and pale rot.
The tide of small lights climbs the stair of bone.
Whatever is written here is written in something living.
The words grow where the words are read.
We are the ink, and the wall is not finished.
Come closer. The glow has been waiting for your breath.
Beneath the roots, a second sky.
Nothing is lost. Everything is being rewritten.
