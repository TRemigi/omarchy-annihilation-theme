The water remembers the shape of whatever fell into it.
Day 3. We are still walking the same direction. The trees disagree.
Something in the reeds is learning our names.
The light does not come from anywhere. It is simply here.
I counted seven of us this morning. I counted six last night.
The moss has begun to grow in patterns I almost recognise.
Every flower on the path is a different flower from the last.
Nothing here is dying. That is the unsettling part.
The sky bends where the water ends.
My reflection arrived a moment after I did.
Do not describe the colours. They will hear you.
Day 9. The notes I wrote yesterday are in a hand I don't know.
The tower goes down, not up.
Static in the radio, and underneath it, breathing.
We were told to expect decay. We found renewal, and it was worse.
The deer has antlers like branching light.
Whatever is changing us is being gentle about it.
Rest. The pond is warm. The pond is patient.
Each cell divides in silence. The silence divides too.
I am no longer sure which of us returned.
